Staying safe on Zynora

Before you meet

  • Keep conversations on Zynora until you trust someone.
  • Video call before meeting in person.
  • Look for the verified badge.

Never send money

No genuine match will ask you for money, gift cards or crypto. Requests like these are the single most common scam pattern — report them.

Meeting in person

  • Meet in a public place the first few times.
  • Tell a friend where you're going and when you expect to be back.
  • Arrange your own transport there and back.

Reporting

Every profile and conversation has a report option. Reports are reviewed by our safety team, and blocking is immediate.
